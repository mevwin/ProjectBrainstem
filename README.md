# Solve Puzzles to Discover your Passions

A 3D puzzle-platformer inspired by ​"The Isle of Four P's" by Ed Hajim.

Play as Marketus, a wandering soul, who arrives at the Island of Passions, a realm that exists beyond Earth. Here, souls arrive to discover their true core commitments. Through a variety of mental challenges, Marketus will learn his foundational passions and depart the island enlightened.

Solve the puzzles inside the ancient shrines. These puzzles will challenge players to experiment with different professions and their respective abilities, each with a unique ability that can be used to approach challenges in different ways.​

[Current version is currently a prototype made for a class project. The game is also available for play on [itch.io](https://mevwin.itch.io/isle-of-passions)]
